Official Rules

The rules sheet.

01 · Match Scoring

Front 9 net Best Ball Match Play = 3 points. Back 9 = 3 points. Overall 18-hole net Best Ball = 5 points. In addition, each hole won earns 1 point.

02 · Birdie & Eagle Bonuses

A gross birdie earns +1 and a gross eagle earns +2, but only when that side wins the hole. If the bonus is tied, the bonus points are split evenly.

03 · Putting

Putts inside the leather are expected to be gimmes. Opponents may require a putt to be holed.

04 · Maximum Score

Maximum score is double par, except par 5s have a maximum of 9. Once a golfer reaches the maximum, they must pick up.

05 · Desert Rule

A ball leaving maintained turf is treated as a lateral hazard. Take lateral relief at the point of entry and add a one-stroke penalty.

06 · Final Round

All Round 5 points are worth 1.5×. The final round pairings are submitted blind.

Handicaps & Tees

Know your number.

How the tournament handicap works

Scottsdale 26 uses a tournament-specific 85% Playing Handicap allowance. Each golfer's GHIN Handicap Index is frozen one week before the trip. That Index is converted to a Course Handicap based on the course and tees being played. The 85% allowance is then applied and rounded to the nearest whole number.

Playing Handicap = Course Handicap × 85% → nearest whole number

The number shown on the scorecard may therefore be different from a golfer's GHIN Handicap Index. Stroke holes are determined from the final Playing Handicap and are used for net scoring in Best Ball and Match Play.
