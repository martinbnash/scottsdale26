The Format

How the championship works.

12 → 2 Teams

Two fixed teams of six compete throughout the event. Each round features three 2-man Best Ball matches.

18 Holes × 3

Every match produces points from the Front 9, Back 9 and the overall 18-hole net Best Ball.

Final Round × 1.5

The final round carries a 1.5× multiplier, making every point count as the championship comes down to the finish.

Official Rules

The rules sheet.

01 · Match Scoring

Front 9 net Best Ball Match Play = 3 points. Back 9 = 3 points. Overall 18-hole net Best Ball = 5 points. In addition, each hole won earns 1 point.

02 · Birdie & Eagle Bonuses

A gross birdie earns +1 and a gross eagle earns +2, but only when that side wins the hole. If the bonus is tied, the bonus points are split evenly.

03 · Putting

Putts inside the leather are expected to be gimmes. Opponents may require a putt to be holed.

04 · Maximum Score

Maximum score is double par, except par 5s have a maximum of 9. Once a golfer reaches the maximum, they must pick up.

05 · Desert Rule

A ball leaving maintained turf is treated as a lateral hazard. Take lateral relief at the point of entry and add a one-stroke penalty.

06 · Final Round

All Round 5 points are worth 1.5×. The final round pairings are submitted blind.

How Scottsdale 26 Handicaps Work

Your GHIN Handicap Index is frozen one week before the tournament. For each round, that Index is converted to a Course Handicap based on the course and tees being played. Scottsdale 26 then applies the 85% tournament allowance, with the result rounded to the nearest whole number.

Playing Handicap = Course Handicap × 85% → nearest whole number

Payouts

There's something on the line.

$250

Entry Fee

Overall event entry for each golfer. With 12 golfers, the entry pool is $3,000.

$50/day

Daily Low Pair

$50 daily pool, split $25/$25 between the winning pair. Five rounds = $250 in daily payouts.

$100

12-Man Parlay

The $100 parlay is separate from the entry pool. If it wins, winnings are split evenly among all 12 golfers. A winning individual pick also gives that golfer's team +1 bonus point.

Overall entry pool: $3,000 from 12 × $250 entries. After $250 in daily low-pair payouts, $2,750 remains for the overall team payout. The $100 12-man parlay is separate.
