Official Rules

How Scottsdale 26 is played.

Match Format

12 golfers are split into two fixed teams of six. Each round consists of three 2-man best-ball matches.

  • Front 9: net match play best ball — 1 point per hole won, plus 3 points for winning the front 9.
  • Back 9: net match play best ball — 1 point per hole won, plus 3 points for winning the back 9.
  • Full 18: net stroke play best ball — 5 points for winning the overall 18.

Handicaps

GHIN Handicap Indexes are frozen one week before the trip. Convert to Course Handicap for the tees being played, then apply the 85% tournament allowance and round to the nearest whole number.

If a golfer does not have a GHIN, the tournament committee assigns a handicap.

Birdie & Eagle Bonuses

Gross birdie = +1 point. Gross eagle = +2 points.

The bonus only applies when that side wins the hole. If a bonus is tied, the bonus points are split evenly.

Maximum Score

Maximum score is double par, except on par 5s where the maximum is 9. Once a golfer reaches the maximum, the ball must be picked up.

Desert / Lateral Rule

A ball leaving maintained turf is treated as a lateral hazard. Take lateral relief at the point of entry with a one-stroke penalty.

Putting Concessions

Putts inside the leather are expected to be gimmes, but an opponent may require the putt to be holed.

Final Round

Final-round points are worth 1.5×. Final-round pairings are submitted blind.

Parlay Bonus

The 12-man parlay is a separate $100 allocation already included in the $300 entry fee. A correct individual parlay pick gives that golfer's team +1 bonus point. If the parlay wins, the winnings are split evenly among all 12 golfers.

* For any rules question or ruling, contact either captain: Clayton or Ryan Hempton.

How To Play

Scorecard instructions.

1

Enter gross scores

On the live scorecard, enter each golfer's gross score for every hole. The scorecard uses the assigned tee and frozen Playing Handicap automatically.

2

Watch the stroke dots

A dot shows the holes where a golfer receives a handicap stroke. Net score = gross score minus the applicable stroke(s).

3

Best Ball drives the match

For each hole, each side uses its lowest net score. That best-ball score determines who wins the hole and the match segments.

4

Three scoring buckets

Every match awards 1 point for each hole won, plus 3 points for the Front 9, 3 for the Back 9, and 5 for the overall 18. Ties split the applicable points; there are no carryovers.

5

Birdies & eagles matter

A gross birdie = +1 and a gross eagle = +2, but only when that side wins the hole. If the bonus is tied, the bonus is split evenly.

6

Just enter the scores

Scores save automatically as they are entered. The live scorecard feeds the match results and team scoreboard, so you do not need a separate Save All Scores button.

7

Certify the scorecard at the end

After all 18 holes are complete, the marker must review the scorecard and check Certified by Marker. Then the opponent reviews the scores and checks Certified by Opponent. When both boxes are checked, the scorecard locks and can no longer be edited. If either box is unchecked, the scorecard remains editable.

What happens after the round: The scorecard calculates net Best Ball results, hole points, Front 9, Back 9, 18-hole points and birdie/eagle bonuses. Round 5 is worth 1.5×, so the final round can swing the championship.
